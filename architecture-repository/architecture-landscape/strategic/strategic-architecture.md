# Strategic Architecture — Northwind Retail Group

> Owner: Chief Architect · Status: Approved · Horizon: 3–5 years · Last reviewed: 2026-02-20

A summary, **enterprise-wide, long-term** view of the architecture. Provides organising
context for segment and capability architectures.

## Strategic intent

Northwind will operate as a **composable, customer-led omnichannel retailer**. By 2030 every
strategic capability will be delivered as a Data Product or composable service that can be
recombined into new customer journeys without bespoke engineering.

## Constraints set at strategic level

- Net carbon zero by 2035 (Group commitment).
- All customer PII processed inside the EU/UK by default.
- No new commitments to single-vendor monoliths over £5m.
