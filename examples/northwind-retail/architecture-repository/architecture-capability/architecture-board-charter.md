# Architecture Board Charter

> Owner: CIO · Status: Approved · Version: 1.4 · Last reviewed: 2026-01-15

## Purpose

The Architecture Board is the standing body responsible for the governance and integrity of
the Northwind Retail Group Enterprise Architecture. It safeguards consistency with the
Architecture Principles and the Tailored Architecture Framework.

## Membership

| Member                              | Role on the Board     | Voting |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------- | :----: |
| Chief Architect                     | Chair                 |   Y    |
| CIO                                 | Sponsor               |   Y    |
| Domain Architect (Business)         | Member                |   Y    |
| Domain Architect (Data)             | Member                |   Y    |
| Domain Architect (Application)      | Member                |   Y    |
| Domain Architect (Technology)       | Member                |   Y    |
| Information Security Architect      | Member                |   Y    |
| EA Council Chair                    | Business voice        |   Y    |
| Programme delivery rep              | Rotating, observer    |   N    |
| Internal Audit                      | Observer              |   N    |

Quorum: Chair + four voting members, including at least one Domain Architect.

## Responsibilities

- Approve Statements of Architecture Work.
- Approve target architectures (ADD) and roadmaps.
- Approve standards additions/changes/retirements.
- Adjudicate compliance reviews and grant waivers.
- Sponsor maturity improvement.

## Decision Rights

| Decision                                  | Authority             |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Approve RfAW                              | Sponsor + Chief Architect |
