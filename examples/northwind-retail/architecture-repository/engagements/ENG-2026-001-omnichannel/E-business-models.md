# Business Models — Omnichannel CX Programme

> Status: Draft · Owner: Daniel Ofori (Domain Architect, Business)
>
> Derived from the SoAW + Vision + Change Request to communicate value to executives.

## Customer Segments

- Existing Northwind customers (loyalty members + casual)
- Net-new digital-first customers in the UK and EU
- Trade customers (small-business buyers in stores)
- Internal customers: store colleagues and contact-centre agents

## Value Propositions

- "Start anywhere, finish anywhere." Cross-channel basket and order continuity.
- "We know you, with your permission." Single, consented customer view.
- "Always-on shelf." Real-time inventory accuracy across channels.
- "Loyalty that recognises you on the spot." Real-time loyalty at the till.
- For colleagues: "One screen, every channel."

## Channels

- Web · Mobile app · In-store POS · Self-service kiosk · Contact centre
- Marketplaces (federated identity-only)
- Email and SMS lifecycle marketing

## Customer Relationships

- Self-service-first across web/app, with one-tap escalation to a human.
- Personalised but consented; granular preference centre.
- "Save my place" persistent journeys across devices.
